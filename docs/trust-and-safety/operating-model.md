# Pinchd trust and safety operating model

## Product capabilities

- Report from profile, message, match and account surfaces.
- Block and unmatch with immediate server-enforced effects.
- Rate limits and abuse controls for likes, messages, account creation and media uploads.
- Profile/photo/text moderation with clear states and appeal routes.
- Scam, spam, impersonation, harassment and threat handling.
- Staff case management with least-privilege access and audit trails.
- Documented escalation paths, response targets and on-call ownership.
- Age eligibility and proportionate risk-based assurance.
- Transparent community standards and user-facing explanations.

## Non-negotiable design rules

- Basic safety controls are free.
- A block must not depend on paid status and must prevent future contact as designed.
- Do not use deceptive profiles or fake activity to make the service appear busier.
- High-impact enforcement needs documented review and appeal paths.
- Moderation evidence is sensitive: access is restricted, audited, and retained only for a defined purpose and period.
- Safety and fairness metrics are reviewed alongside growth and revenue metrics.

## Operational metrics

Track report volume and severity, response time by severity, repeat-offender rate, block effectiveness, appeal reversal rate, false positives, user trust feedback, and staff queue health. Interpret raw report rates with care because increases can reflect either more harm or better reporting.
